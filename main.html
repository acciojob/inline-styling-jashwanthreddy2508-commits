Flowers photos

Sunflowers (Helianthus annuus) are tall, vibrant annuals in the aster family known for large, composite flower heads. Native to North America, they are famous for heliotropism, where immature buds track the sun, and for producing edible seeds and commercial oils.

Roses pictures

A rose is a woody perennial flowering plant of the genus Rosa characterized by showy petals, prickly stems, and a sweet fragrance. There are over 300 species and thousands of cultivated varieties ranging from small bushes to climbing vines.
